What You'll Learn
Principle 01
Transparency
Affected parties must know when AI is deciding their futures — and have a way to contest it.
Principle 02
Accountability
Responsibility cannot be diffused across an AI supply chain. Deployers own a share of every outcome.
Principle 03
Auditability
AI systems must produce records sufficient for independent review. Harm that can't be traced can't be remedied.
